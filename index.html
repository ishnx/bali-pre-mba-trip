Day by day

The plan as the organizing committee has it today, line by line.

DAY 1

Monday 30 Nov - Arrive and settle in

Seminyak
  • Check-in from 1 PM at the villa, with a meet-and-greet, welcome mimosas and welcome gifts.
  • Lunch at a nearby cafe for whoever has arrived, then rest at the villa.
  • Late dinner and drinks at Revolver, the Seminyak institution a short ride away.
  • After: La Favela for the ones with energy left, or rest and recharge.
Heads-up: several people land at Denpasar airport around 7:20 AM, and the host has declined early check-in. The group still needs a plan for that six-hour gap - likely luggage drop plus a cafe base.
DAY 2

Tuesday 1 Dec - Ubud adventure day

Ubud
  • Light breakfast at the villa (being arranged), then leave at 11 AM - about an hour's ride to Ubud.
  • Main activity: ATV rides or group river rafting - both proved popular in the signup.
  • After: the Bali swing or the Cretya day club; temples for whoever opts in.
  • Late lunch in Ubud - options still being scouted.
  • Back in Seminyak by 8-8:30 PM for dinner at the villa and group games.
Pack a change of clothes for this day. ATV and rafting still need pre-booking for a group this size.
DAY 3

Wednesday 2 Dec - FINNS or Luna beach club

Canggu
  • Brunch at Crate Cafe on the way to Canggu.
  • Beach club, 1-2 PM to 8 PM: FINNS Beach Club or its sister venue Luna - the package is not chosen yet (see costs).
  • Evening: recovery mode, Grab back, food at a nearby cafe.
Nothing booked yet. The cheap packages lock in a before-1 PM arrival on what the itinerary itself calls a hangover day; the flexible whole-group cabana costs about four times more per person.
DAY 4

Thursday 3 Dec - Free day and Mexicola night

Seminyak / Canggu
  • Late, light breakfast at the villa.
  • Lunch 1 PM at Milk & Madu Berawa - a walk-in, so the group just shows up hungry.
  • Free afternoon: Kuta beach, surf classes, shopping, or doing nothing by the pool.
  • Dinner 8 PM - venue still blank in the plan.
  • Afterparty route: Motel Mexicola, then Desa Kitsune, then Old Man's or Luigi's.
DAY 5

Friday 4 Dec - Savaya sunset

Uluwatu
  • Brunch at the villa or nearby, free morning.
  • Leave 3-3:30 PM for Savaya, the cliff-top club in Uluwatu - roughly 1.5 hours from Seminyak.
  • Table combo not chosen yet - six options priced in the plan (see costs).
Nothing booked yet, and moving the whole group to Uluwatu and back needs its own transport plan - currently just "Grab or rent a scooty" in the notes.
DAY 6

Saturday 5 Dec - Checkout

Seminyak
  • Last brunch together, timed around people's flights.
  • Checkout. Late checkout was requested and declined, so plan to be out on time.

Where we stay
